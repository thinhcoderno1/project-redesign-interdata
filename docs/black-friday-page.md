# Black Friday

- Route: `/black-friday/` inside the existing `(website)` layout. Shared Navigation, Footer, font, container widths and floating contact remain in use.
- Styling is scoped to `src/components/black-friday/black-friday.module.css`. The dark, blue and orange palette and geometric hero follow the supplied reference; the hero artwork is live text and CSS, without a raster dependency.
- Best Plans displays only the first three VPS Inter Platinum products from existing group 100. Its eyebrow, title, description and billing controls are centered. Billing tabs follow the pill design of `/thue-vps/`, with 1 year, 2 years and 3 years and arrow/Home/End keyboard support. Existing validators, specs, full-cycle prices and checkout URLs are reused. Platinum Cloud group 41 remains available only for the separate Cloud Flash Deal card.
- Prices are **full cycle totals**, never a monthly conversion. Only supported billing cycles are offered; the checkout URL carries the selected cycle. Failed pricing requests render a contact path without fabricated prices.
- Best Plans defaults to 3 years, including server rendering without JavaScript. Its crossed-out originals reuse `hotDeals.originalPrices` from `src/data/vps-content.json`, matched by Inter Platinum product name and selected cycle. Savings badges replace the Black Friday tags and show `floor((original - current) / original * 100)`; invalid/missing originals or current prices, other product groups, or prices at/above the original suppress both the original and savings badge. These originals are retained project source data, not additional original-price fields from the live catalog API. Current prices and checkout remain live and unchanged.
- Best Plans initially shows CPU, RAM and NVMe. Each card has its own native details control to expand/collapse IPv4, network port and unlimited bandwidth, including without JavaScript. All configuration lines retain their source text and matching icons. The renewal-price statement is excluded by the existing Hot Deals parser. Flash Sale retains its compact configuration summary.
- The 16px original-price line includes the selected period in months inside the same strike-through, e.g. `8.409.600đ / 36 tháng`, using 12, 24 or 36 months without a separate original-price label. The billing tabs retain their 1-year, 2-year and 3-year labels.
- Each Best Plans card shows a red gradient gift tag, “TẶNG THÊM 1 NĂM”, immediately after the short description only when the 3-year billing cycle is selected. The 1-year and 2-year cycles omit it. This user-supplied benefit does not alter the selected billing cycle, totals or checkout URL.
- Renewal sits between Best Plans and Flash Sale and follows the supplied reference: a “Gia hạn dịch vụ.” heading, orange eyebrow and Black Friday badge, blue 50% card with a decorative renewal arrow and white CTA, and white 10% card with orange offer text and blue CTA. The content is centered at a maximum width of 980px, with compact heading, offer numbers, card padding and buttons. Conditions and the existing customer login URLs are retained. Cards sit side by side on desktop and stack below 700px. The section navigation includes Renewal and the section numbers follow the four-section order.
- Best Plans card 2 retains its blue outline; the “Gợi ý cho bạn” banner and its reserved mobile gap are removed.
- Flash Sale has the three time windows supplied in the reference. Switching a window updates the schedule and the contextual CTA label. The product cards use existing catalog prices; no additional discount, stock quantity, depletion counter or live countdown is fabricated. CTAs go to consultation until campaign-specific products and dates are supplied.
- Coupon percentages and durations match the supplied reference and are centralized in `src/data/black-friday.ts`. These are presentation inputs, not verified commercial campaign terms. No redeemable codes were supplied: the cards link to consultation instead of copying a fake code. Native details show the terms and link to the matching service page.
- The coupon section follows the latest reference: large title and Black Friday badge, VPS/Cloud icons and two service columns, blue highlighted 3-year cards, white 2-year/1-year cards with orange discount numbers, and dashed vertical separators before the code/action area. Conditions sit below each group in native details. Without a real code the dashed field reads “Liên hệ nhận mã” and the existing “Nhận ưu đãi” CTA still links to contact. No mock code, inactive copy control or launch note is shown. Columns stack at 900px and below.
- Sales handoff: replace reference discounts/time windows with approved values, provide eligible products, dates/timezone, real redemption codes, renewal terms and availability before publishing the campaign. No demo/placeholder notes appear in the rendered page.
- The route inherits the project's current noindex policy. This work does not publish or deploy the page.

## Verification

```powershell
npm run build
node scripts/verify-black-friday.mjs
```

Use `TEST_BASE_URL` to check another local server. The script verifies VPS-only yearly tabs, keyboard support, cycle-specific checkout URLs, centered Best Plans alignment, time-window selection, coupon terms, section anchors, no-JavaScript rendering, seven viewport widths (320–1920), browser errors and WCAG A/AA with Axe. Screenshots and machine-readable results are in `artifacts/black-friday/` (Git ignored).
