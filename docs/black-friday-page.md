# Black Friday

- Route: `/blackfriday/` inside the existing `(website)` layout. `/black-friday/` permanently redirects to it (308), preserving query parameters. Canonical, Open Graph URL and structured data use `/blackfriday/`. Shared Navigation, Footer, font, container widths and floating contact remain in use.
- Best Plans uses the VPS Platinum Black Friday design from the demo. It displays four cards per row on desktop, two on tablet and a swipeable list on phones. P2 carries the “Phù hợp nhất” badge.
- The campaign catalog comes from `https://support.interdata.vn/packages.php?gid=105&module` and revalidates every 60 seconds. The loader validates the group, the eight P1–P8 products (PIDs 570–577) and their checkout URLs. The live API sets the full price for each offered billing cycle. A missing cycle or unavailable catalog provides a contact link.
- The configuration is displayed as a seven-row list with icons. The loader reads CPU, RAM, NVMe, network details, firewall and eligible gifts from Portal and formats them for the cards. Gifts appear for P2–P8 on the 3-year tab. That tab is selected by default and all three tabs support arrow, Home and End keys.
- Crossed-out amounts show the VPS Platinum Viettel reference price for matching resources. Where the demo uses an estimate, the amount uses the demo’s resource rates and is marked as estimated in its accessible label and tooltip.
- Flash Sale is visible immediately with `blackFridayFlashSale.mode: "preview"`. The countdown targets `2026-11-23T11:00:00+07:00` (11:00 on November 23, 2026, Vietnam time) and uses server time plus elapsed monotonic browser time. `/api/black-friday-flash-sale/` returns `private, no-store` responses and uses only server time to authorize code release. An open page checks for codes at the opening time and continues checking while awaiting codes. `mode: "hidden"` hides the section and blocks code release.
- The page uses `src/components/black-friday/black-friday.module.css`, with a separate CSS module for Flash Sale. Both sections use the dark, blue and orange palette from their references.
- Best Plans, Renewal and Coupons H2 headings share one responsive 28-40px font size and display in uppercase. Flash Sale has its own heading typography from the supplied mockup.
- Flash Sale and Coupons share one dark #080e12 backdrop. The blue and orange glows continue across the boundary between the sections.
- A blue consultation CTA follows Coupons, reusing the same `ConsultationCta` component and `consultation-woman-upper-body.webp` artwork as S3 Object Storage. It keeps the shared blue gradient, rounded card, orbit and three decorative icons. Black Friday supplies its own copy, the page's uppercase H2 size without an eyebrow, an InterData Facebook link (`https://www.facebook.com/interdata.com.vn`) and a return link to `#best-plans`. An empty eyebrow prop omits its markup for Black Friday; existing S3 and other CTA instances keep their original appearance.
- The hero, Best Plans, Renewal and Coupons eyebrow labels share the local `EyebrowBadge` component. Flash Sale uses the Black Friday 2026 eyebrow from the mockup.
- Renewal sits between Best Plans and Flash Sale. Its current desktop order is the “Gia hạn. Thêm lợi ích.” intro with one “Gia hạn ngay” CTA, then up to 50% off, then up to 6 extra months. The CTA uses `contactDetails.facebook` (`https://www.facebook.com/interdata.com.vn`); offer columns have no separate CTAs. `blackFridayRenewal` in `src/data/black-friday.ts` controls temporary visibility: `showDescriptions: false` hides explanatory copy including the 31/08/2026 cutoff, while `showExtraDiscount: false` hides the additional 10% offer. Both sets of content remain in JSX and can be restored independently by setting the respective flag to `true`; restoring the 10% offer also restores the fourth desktop column. The section preserves its full-width blue/datacenter background, shared container, typography, icons and thin separators. Above 1000px it has three columns; at 601–1000px the intro spans a row above two side-by-side offers; at 600px and below all content stacks in the same reading order. The section anchor and neighboring sections are unchanged.
- Renewal uses three equal-width desktop columns with matching horizontal padding and centered content, distributing whitespace evenly around the intro and two offers. The optional fourth column also uses an equal share when enabled. Tablet columns retain symmetric padding and centered content; compact mobile rows keep their left-aligned icon/text layout.
- The renewal CTA is a transparent button-style link with a white border, white text, 6px corners and a 44px minimum height. Hover and keyboard focus switch it to a white fill with dark brand-blue text, using a short color transition that respects reduced motion. Its position and Facebook destination are unchanged.
- Renewal's “50%” and “6 THÁNG” use the same `--renewal-amount-size` for their main numerals at every breakpoint, with a shared line-height and baseline. Their visible amount rows align at both the top and bottom on desktop/tablet; stacked mobile offers retain equal numeral heights. The previous larger font overrides on the six-month amount are removed.
- The Flash Sale countdown sits above the 0đ .COM domain ticket and six VPS and Cloud discount tickets. The six discount tickets show “Chu kỳ 3 tháng – 1 năm”. At opening, each ticket displays its quantity above the copy button. Public details and terms are available without JavaScript.
- Flash Sale's native “Điều kiện áp dụng” disclosure lists five user-supplied terms from `blackFridayFlashSaleConditions` in `src/data/black-friday.ts`: first-payment-only ONE TIME codes and cycle guidance; priority for early payments; deletion of invoices unpaid after 15 minutes; no simultaneous/stacked promotions; and no credit-account payments. The support-contact bullet has been removed. The compact bullet list works without JavaScript; the two service configuration links have been removed. Coupon-section terms are unchanged.
- The November 23 codes and quantities live in `src/lib/black-friday-flash-sale.ts`. The release API sends an empty `offers` object up to opening time, then sends each offer's code and advertised quantity together. An open page picks up the release automatically.
- Each Flash Sale offer with a validated checkout link shows “Đăng ký ngay” below the code field. The Black Friday server page reads the VPS Platinum Viettel catalog (group 101) and the Platinum G2 Cloud catalog (group 41) in parallel. The checkout resolver matches the exact product IDs, slugs and storefront paths and opens checkout with the 3-month cycle selected. The three Cloud links go to the HCM plans. Codes release independently of the catalog requests.
- Coupon discounts and explicit billing durations are defined in `src/data/black-friday.ts`: VPS receives 45% / 50% / 55% for 12 / 24 / 36 months; Cloud Server receives 30% / 35% / 40% for the same cycles. All are ONE TIME, applying only to the first payment. Each service's native details shows only the three supplied conditions, without the repeated cycle/discount mappings or configuration links: first-payment-only codes and cycle guidance, no stacked promotions, and no payment through InterData credit balances.
- Coupon code definitions are stored only in `src/lib/black-friday-coupons.ts`, protected by `import "server-only"`. `publishCouponCodes` is `true` at the user's request. `getBlackFridayCoupons()` publishes VPS codes `BF26-VPS55` / `BF26-VPS50` / `BF26-VPS45` and Cloud Server codes `BF26-CLOUD40` / `BF26-CLOUD35` / `BF26-CLOUD30` for 36 / 24 / 12 months respectively. The server page passes these display values into the client component and HTML/RSC. Setting the flag to `false` restores `BF26-XXXXX` placeholders. These regular coupons are unaffected by Flash Sale release timing.
- The coupon section retains its two service columns, blue highlighted 3-year cards, white 2-year/1-year cards, dashed code fields and “Áp mã ngay” links to the respective VPS/Cloud Server hubs. Each code field displays its published service/cycle code. Columns stack at 900px and below. Apply links retain their existing destinations.
- Both Flash Sale and Coupons use `CouponCopyButton`: the displayed code and copy icon share one keyboard-accessible button. Regular coupons are always available; Flash Sale copy buttons mount only after server-authorized release. Success changes the icon to a check and announces “Đã sao chép” in a temporary live status bubble. Denied/unavailable clipboard access reports an error without a false success. Feedback resets after 2.5 seconds and its timer is cleaned up on unmount.
- Sales handoff: configure eligible products, billing cycles and actual redemption limits in the sales system. The frontend controls display and publication timing, not checkout coupon validation or inventory.
- The route inherits the project's public index/follow policy. This work does not publish or deploy the page.

## Verification

The FAQ section follows the consultation CTA and includes eight native details/summary accordions. Its light, rounded panel uses a centered blue uppercase heading, an orange icon badge and a subtle CSS geometric backdrop. Questions are split into two independent columns of four, with thin separators and circular plus/minus controls; below 801px they stack into one column in the original reading order. All answers start collapsed. The former sidebar and its contact button are removed; the eight questions and existing answer links remain in place. The cycle-selection answer objectively compares 1/2/3 years, recommends 3 years for stable long-term needs and lists the gifts available with a 3-year order. Keyboard focus is visible and the accordions work without JavaScript. The heading retains the page-wide H2 font size.
